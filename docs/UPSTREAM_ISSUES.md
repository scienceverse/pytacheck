# Upstream issues and deliberate differences

pytacheck is checked against metacheck under the accuracy contract
([PORTING.md](PORTING.md), section 1): at least as accurate as metacheck on real
inputs, with metacheck's bugs fixed rather than reproduced. This page is the register
of every difference. It collects the problems found in metacheck (`dev` plus
pull request [#423](https://github.com/scienceverse/metacheck/pull/423), bibr export
schema 12.0, as pinned in `parity/UPSTREAM.toml`) so they can be reported upstream,
and records what pytacheck does about each. Parity marks cite its entries
(`known_divergence: {kind, ref, reason}`, see [PARITY.md](PARITY.md)), and
`tests/foundation/test_upstream_issues.py` checks that every cited entry exists and
that its status agrees with the marks.

* **Deliberate differences** (D-entries) are design choices where pytacheck does
  something metacheck does not, or does it differently on purpose. Marks of kind
  `better_logic` and `deliberate` cite them.
* **Upstream issues** (U-entries) are bugs in metacheck or its dependencies. Each entry
  describes metacheck's behaviour and ends with what pytacheck does about it, which
  its **status** column sums up:

| status | pytacheck | marks citing the entry |
|---|---|---|
| fixed | does the right thing. **Fixed in pytacheck** says what, and names the parity cases that show it. | `kind: r_bug_fixed` on the cases that differ from R. A fix that only corrects text (a typo, a plural) adds `r_text` substitutions to the mark, and the case is still compared with R for everything else. |
| partly fixed | fixes part of the entry and keeps the rest; **Kept** gives the reason. | as for fixed |
| kept | reproduces metacheck's behaviour. **Kept** says why: it cannot be seen, it is harmless, or fixing it would make results worse. | no `r_bug_fixed` marks |
| n/a | has nothing to fix: the entry concerns metacheck alone (its `DESCRIPTION`, its tests, dead code pytacheck does not port). **Not applicable** says why. | no `r_bug_fixed` marks |
| open | has not handled it yet. | no `r_bug_fixed` marks |

Right-sizing lanes add their entries only in their own section at the end of this
page, within the IDs reserved for them ([PORTING.md](PORTING.md), section 6). The
tables above skip those IDs, so the U-entry after U158 is U195.
